I'm a quantitative researcher at Prudential Financial, working in portfolio analytics across emerging markets — building models that support asset allocation, risk measurement and investment decisions.

Before Prudential I led data science delivery at Arundo Analytics, worked as a data scientist at QRI in oil & gas value creation, and spent a decade at Pemex as a quantitative advisor to the CFO — forecasting prices, tariffs and P&L, and running a procurement optimization desk.

I hold a PhD in Mathematics from the University of Liverpool (Institute for Financial and Actuarial Mathematics), an MS in Computer Science from Georgia Tech, and BS degrees in Actuarial Sciences and Applied Mathematics from ITAM. My research focuses on the long-term financial sustainability of pension systems in aging economies.
